# 26-Week Master Schedule (Zero → N2)

Study 6 days a week, 5–6 hours a day. Day 7 is a light review day (Anki only, 1 hour).

References in this schedule:
- **Kanji Day N** = the "Day N" set in the matching kanji file in [02-kanji/](../02-kanji/)
- **Vocab #a–b** = entry numbers in the matching vocab file in [03-vocabulary/](../03-vocabulary/)
- **Grammar #a–b** = numbered points in the matching grammar file in [04-grammar/](../04-grammar/)

Every week ends with a **Saturday self-test**: rewrite the week's kanji from memory, do a 50-word vocab quiz from Anki, and write 10 sentences using the week's grammar.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation and N5 (Weeks 1–5)

Goal: read kana fluently, know 100 kanji, 700 words, all N5 grammar.

| Week | Kanji | Vocabulary | Grammar | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Kana speed drill daily (target: read a 100-char kana passage in under 60 s). N5 Kanji Days 1–4 | N5 #121–260 | N5 Grammar #1–20 (です, particles は/が/を/に/へ/で, question forms) | Numbers to 10,000, counters, dates, time |
| 3 | N5 Kanji Days 5–8 | N5 #261–410 | N5 Grammar #21–45 (verb groups, ます forms, て-form, adjectives) | Start Anki reviews every morning (30 min) |
| 4 | N5 Kanji Days 9–10 + review all 100 | N5 #411–560 | N5 Grammar #46–65 | Start listening block: Comprehensible Japanese beginner videos, 30 min/day |
| 5 | Review all N5 kanji; write each from memory | N5 #561–700 | N5 Grammar #66–end; review entire N5 file | **Checkpoint**: write a 150-character self-introduction using N5 grammar. Read N4-level passages 1–2 in [passages-N4-N3.md](../05-reading/passages-N4-N3.md) |

## Phase 5 — Exam preparation (Weeks 25–26)

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 25 | Weak-point week. For every wrong answer on Mock A: reread the grammar point or kanji set, redo 10 Anki reps. Re-read all 16 N2 passages at exam speed (短文 3 min, 中文 8 min, 長文 12 min). Listen to every listening script twice without the text. |
| 26 | **MOCK TEST N2-B** on Monday under full timing. Tuesday–Thursday: review errors. Friday: light review of confusable pairs and contractions only. Saturday: rest. Target 65%+ on Mock B. If below 55%, add 4 more weeks using the official JLPT 問題集 (see resources). |

## After week 26 (if your exam is July 2027)

Keep Anki reviews daily (45 min). Each week: 1 official practice test section, 3 NHK news items, 2 podcast episodes. Reread N2 grammar once a month. Do not stop listening.
