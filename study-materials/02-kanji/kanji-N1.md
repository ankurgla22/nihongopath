# JLPT N1 Kanji — Complete Study List (600 kanji, 50 days)

Scope: 600 of the most frequent kanji introduced at the N1 level (beyond N5–N2). 12 kanji per day for 50 days. Kanji already covered in kanji-N5.md, kanji-N4.md, kanji-N3.md or kanji-N2.md are not repeated here. On-yomi in katakana, kun-yomi in hiragana (okurigana in parentheses). "—" means no common reading of that type.
