# JLPT N2 Kanji — Complete Study List (387 kanji, 33 days)

Scope: kanji introduced at the N2 level (beyond N5/N4/N3). 12 kanji per day for 33 days (Day 25 has 9, Day 33 has 6). Days 26–33 fill the remaining gaps in the standard N2/N3 kanji lists, bringing the N5–N2 cumulative total to about 1,000. Kanji already covered in kanji-N3.md are not repeated here. On-yomi in katakana, kun-yomi in hiragana (okurigana in parentheses). "—" means no common reading of that type.
