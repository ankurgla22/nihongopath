# JLPT N4 Kanji — Complete List (148 kanji)

This is the standard N4 kanji set (the widely used unofficial N4 list plus 引・弱・光・声, which appear on most other N4 lists), minus the 22 kanji already covered in kanji-N5.md. All 103 N5 kanji are assumed known and are not repeated here.

**How to use:** one set per day (10 kanji). Write each kanji 10 times, read both example words aloud, then cover the columns and self-test. Review Day *n-1* and Day *n-7* each morning. After Day 15, cycle through all 148 in random order until you can produce reading + meaning in under 3 seconds each.

Column key: On-yomi in katakana, kun-yomi in hiragana (okurigana in parentheses). "—" means the reading is not needed at N4 level.

## Reading patterns worth memorizing

| Pattern | Rule | Examples from this list |
|---|---|---|
| Sound change (rendaku) | Second element voiced: か→が, し→じ, た→だ, は→ば | 手紙 てがみ, 悪口 わるぐち, 大声 おおごえ |

### How to use the radicals

1. When you meet a new kanji, split it into radical + remainder (e.g. 海 = 氵water + 毎). The radical usually hints at the meaning field (水 → liquids, 言 → speech, 心 → feelings, 辶 → movement).
2. Many "remainder" parts hint at the on-yomi: 青 → セイ (晴・清・精), 生 → セイ (性・姓), 交 → コウ (校・効), 反 → ハン (飯・板・坂), 同 → ドウ (銅・胴), 寺 → ジ (時・持), 白 → ハク (拍・泊), 反・返・飯・板 all carry ハン. Collecting these phonetic components saves enormous time at N3/N2.
3. Confusable pairs to watch: 待 vs 持 (彳 going vs 扌 hand), 住 vs 注 (亻 vs 氵), 貸 vs 借 (貝 shell below vs 亻 left), 験 vs 検/険, 週 vs 周, 有 vs 右, 図 vs 区, 分 vs 今, 集 vs 進.
