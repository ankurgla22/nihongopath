# JLPT N3 Vocabulary — Part 1 (#1–500)

Words beyond N5/N4 level that appear on the N3 test. Part 1 covers: Society & news, Work & business, Education, Health & body, Emotions & personality, Relationships, Daily life & housing, Food & cooking, Travel & transport, Nature & environment, Technology.

See also: `vocab-N3-part2.md` (#501–1000) and `vocab-N3-part3.md` (#1001–1500).

Abbreviations: n = noun, vs = suru-verb, vt = transitive verb, vi = intransitive verb, i-adj = い-adjective, na-adj = な-adjective, adv = adverb, conj = conjunction, exp = expression.
