# JLPT N3 Vocabulary — Part 2 (#501–1000)

Words beyond N5/N4 level that appear on the N3 test. Part 2 covers: Economy & money, Law & rules, Culture & arts, Sports, Abstract nouns, Katakana words, Set phrases & idioms, and Suru-verbs (A).

See also: `vocab-N3-part1.md` (#1–500) and `vocab-N3-part3.md` (#1001–1500).

Abbreviations: n = noun, vs = suru-verb, vt = transitive verb, vi = intransitive verb, i-adj = い-adjective, na-adj = な-adjective, adv = adverb, conj = conjunction, exp = expression.
