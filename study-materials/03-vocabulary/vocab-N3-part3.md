# JLPT N3 Vocabulary — Part 3 (#1001–1500)

Words beyond N5/N4 level that appear on the N3 test. Part 3 covers: Suru-verbs (B), Verbs (transitive/intransitive pairs, compound verbs, other verbs), い-adjectives, な-adjectives, Adverbs, Onomatopoeia, and Conjunctions.

See also: `vocab-N3-part1.md` (#1–500) and `vocab-N3-part2.md` (#501–1000).

Abbreviations: n = noun, vs = suru-verb, vt = transitive verb, vi = intransitive verb, i-adj = い-adjective, na-adj = な-adjective, adv = adverb, conj = conjunction, exp = expression.
