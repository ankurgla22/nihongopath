# N2 聴解 (Listening) Guide

The listening section is the part most self-studiers underestimate. It is 60 of the 180 total points, it is the last section of the day (you will be tired), and unlike reading you cannot go back. This guide covers the structure, strategies, the spoken language you must recognise instantly, and a daily routine.

---

## 1. Structure of the N2 聴解 section

| # | 問題 | Name | Questions | Time per item | What you hear | When you see the options |
|---|------|------|-----------|---------------|---------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | 問題1 | 課題理解 (task-based comprehension) | 5 | ~1.5 min | Situation → question → dialogue → question repeated | Options printed on paper; read them BEFORE the audio |
| 2 | 問題2 | ポイント理解 (point comprehension) | 6 | ~2 min | Situation → question → **20-second pause to read options** → dialogue/monologue → question repeated | Printed; you get a pause to read them |
| 3 | 問題3 | 概要理解 (summary/gist comprehension) | 5 | ~1.5 min | Situation (brief) → monologue or dialogue → question → 4 spoken options | Nothing printed. Question and options are spoken only |
| 4 | 問題4 | 即時応答 (quick response) | 12 | ~20 sec | One short utterance → 3 spoken replies | Nothing printed. 3 options only |
| 5 | 問題5 | 統合理解 (integrated comprehension) | 4 (2 single items + 1 two-part item) | ~3 min | Long dialogue/monologue with multiple pieces of information → question(s) | 5-1 and 5-2: nothing printed. 5-3: options printed for both sub-questions |

- **Total: 32 questions, approximately 50 minutes** (the audio runs continuously; there is no "extra" time at the end).
- The instructions and one 例 (example) are played before each 問題. Use the example time to read ahead.
- Everything is played **once only**.
- Answers are marked on the mark sheet with a pencil. There is no penalty for wrong answers, so never leave a blank.

### Scoring

- The listening section is scored separately: **0–60 points, scaled**. Raw correct answers are converted using item response theory, so 20/32 does not equal 37.5/60 exactly, but as a working rule assume **roughly 60% correct ≈ 36–38/60**.
- Pass condition for N2: **total ≥ 90/180 AND each section ≥ 19/60**. 19/60 is a low bar (about 10–12 correct out of 32), but do not aim there: listening is the easiest section in which to pick up points once you have a routine, and a strong listening score offsets a weak reading section.
- Realistic target for a safe pass: **40+/60 in listening**.

## 2. How each part works and the strategy for it

### 問題1 課題理解 — "What does the person do next?"

**Format**: A man and a woman talk (office, school, shop, home). The question is always about an action: 「男の人はこのあとまず何をしますか」「女の人は何を持って行かなければなりませんか」「二人は何を買いますか」.

**Key features**:
- The question is read *before* the dialogue, so you know what to listen for.
- The dialogue almost always contains **rejected candidates**: things that were considered and dropped ("A も要るよね" → "あ、A はもう頼んでおいた"). The answer is what is left after the eliminations.
- Listen for **order words**: まず、先に、その前に、それから、後で、〜てから、〜たら.
- Listen for **change of plan**: あ、やっぱり / そういえば / じゃあ / 〜はいいや / 〜はもういい / それは私がやっておく.

**Strategy**:
1. During the instructions, read all 4 options for question 1. They are usually 4 actions or 4 items.
2. As the dialogue plays, put a small × next to any option that is rejected, and ○ next to what is confirmed. Write "先" beside the thing that must be done first.
3. Answer immediately after the second reading of the question, then move your eyes to the next question's options during the pause.

### 問題2 ポイント理解 — "Why / which / what is the reason?"

**Format**: Situation → question → **20 seconds of silence** → dialogue or monologue → question again. Typical questions: 「どうして〜ましたか」「何が一番の理由ですか」「〜は何だと言っていますか」「男の人は何について困っていますか」.

**Key features**:
- Multiple reasons are mentioned; typically only one is the "real" or "main" reason (一番の理由). The speaker often says "それもあるけど、一番は…" or "本当は…".
- Distractors are things that *sound* like reasons but are dismissed: 「〜と思われがちだけど」「〜というわけではなくて」.
- Listen for evaluative language at the end: 「やっぱり〜が大きい」「結局〜」「〜が決め手だった」.

**Strategy**:
1. Use the full 20 seconds to read the options. Underline the key noun/verb in each.
2. Note which speaker the question is about (男 or 女) — options are often things the *other* speaker said.
3. Do not choose the first reason mentioned. The correct answer tends to appear in the last third of the dialogue.

### 問題3 概要理解 — "What is the main point?"

**Format**: Very short situation (「テレビで男の人が話しています」), then a monologue (or dialogue) of 40–60 seconds, then the question, then 4 spoken options. Nothing is printed.

**Typical questions**: 「男の人は何について話していますか」「話のテーマは何ですか」「女の人が一番言いたいことは何ですか」「この商品の特徴は何ですか」.

**Key features**:
- You must grasp the *theme* and the *speaker's stance*, not details.
- The conclusion is often marked: 「つまり」「このように」「ですから」「〜べきだと思います」「〜が大切です」.
- Options are abstract nouns/phrases: 「新しい制度の問題点」「〜の必要性」「〜の方法」.

**Strategy**:
1. Write 3–5 keywords as you listen (topic, who, positive/negative, main verb).
2. Note the final sentence — it very often contains the answer.
3. When the options are read, write 1–4 with a ○/×/△ beside each. Pick the ○, or the least-crossed if unsure.
4. Do not get distracted by an option that repeats a word you heard: that is a common trap.

### 問題4 即時応答 — quick response

**Format**: One person says one sentence (question, request, offer, complaint, apology…). Three responses are read. Choose the most natural. 12 items, roughly 20 seconds each. Nothing printed.

**Key features**:
- Tests pragmatics: keigo direction (who is being humble/respectful), tense/aspect, negative questions, set phrases, and spoken contractions.
- Traps: a response that uses the same word as the prompt but the wrong function; a response that answers a different question type (a yes/no answer to a "which" question); wrong keigo direction (using 尊敬語 about yourself).

**Strategy**:
1. Identify the **function** of the prompt in the first second: is it an offer? a request? a complaint? See the 50-pattern table in §5.
2. Decide the expected response type ("accept/decline", "apologise", "reassure", "give permission") *before* the options play.
3. Mark immediately. If two remain, choose the one whose keigo direction matches. Never dwell: the next item starts in seconds.

### 問題5 統合理解 — integrating information

**Format**:
- **5-1 and 5-2**: a longer dialogue (often 3 speakers: e.g. a shop clerk explaining four products, then a couple deciding) or a monologue followed by a two-person discussion. One question each, nothing printed.
- **5-3**: A monologue (e.g. a radio or in-store announcement describing four options: courses, tours, apartments, plans), then a couple discuss which to choose. **Two questions**: 質問1 which does the man choose, 質問2 which does the woman choose. Options for both are printed on paper.

**Key features**:
- You must hold 3–4 items with 2–3 attributes each, then match them against the speakers' preferences.
- Speakers often change their minds ("最初は A がいいと思ったけど、やっぱり B にする").
- Reasons are stated, then a choice follows.

**Strategy**:
1. Draw a quick grid: rows = items (1–4), columns = attributes (price, time, place, feature). Fill in with abbreviations as the announcement plays.
2. When the couple talk, write 男 and 女 with arrows to the item each ends up with. Cross out earlier preferences when they change.
3. For 5-3, read the printed options during the instructions so you know what the 4 items will be.

## 3. What to note-take (and what not to)

Notes must be minimal — writing while listening costs comprehension. Use these habits:

| Write | Symbol/abbreviation |
|-------|--------------------|
| Speaker | 男 / 女 / 店 (clerk) / 先 (teacher) / 客 |
| Numbers, times, dates, prices | Digits: 3時, 500, 10/5, 月 (Monday) |
| Sequence | ①②③, or → |
| Rejected item | × |
| Confirmed | ○ |
| Uncertain | △ |
| Positive / negative opinion | + / − |
| Change of mind | ✓ old → new |
| Reason | ∵ (because) or "り" |
| Not needed / already done | 済 |

Do NOT: write whole words in kanji, translate into your native language, or try to note every detail. For 問題3 and 問題5, the best note is a 4-row grid; for 問題1 the best note is ○/× on the printed options; for 問題4 write nothing at all.

**A common failure**: looking at the mark sheet while the next question's situation is being read. Mark quickly, then eyes up.

| 50 | Asking for a favour with 〜ていただきたいんですが | 推薦状を書いていただきたいんですが。 | Agree/ask details | いいですよ。いつまでに？ | 「書いていただきます」 |

**Rule of thumb for keigo direction in 即時応答**: 尊敬語 (いらっしゃる、おっしゃる、ご覧になる、召し上がる、なさる) is about *the other person or a superior*; 謙譲語 (参る、申す、拝見する、いただく、いたす、伺う) is about *yourself or your in-group*. If a response describes the listener's boss with 謙譲語 or the speaker with 尊敬語, it is wrong.

## 7. Shadowing method

Shadowing = repeating the audio *as it plays*, a beat behind, matching rhythm, pitch, and speed. It trains the ear to parse fast speech and builds the muscle memory that makes contractions feel natural.

**Material**: 30–60 second clips at or slightly below your level with a transcript (Nihongo con Teppei, NHK Easy read-aloud, JLPT sample dialogues, the scripts in `listening-scripts.md` fed through TTS).

**Procedure (15 minutes, one clip)**:
1. **Listen blind** ×2. Write what you understood in one line.
2. **Read the transcript** silently, look up nothing unknown yet; just mark words you missed.
3. **Listen while reading** ×2. Notice where sounds contract (ている→てる, 〜ておく→とく).
4. **Look up** the 2–3 words that blocked meaning. No more.
5. **Mumble-shadow** ×3: read along quietly with the audio, staying in sync.
6. **Full shadow without text** ×3: audio on, eyes closed, repeat with a half-second lag. Do not stop when you stumble; rejoin at the next phrase.
7. **Record yourself** once and compare with the original for pitch and rhythm (optional but effective).
8. **Next day**: shadow the same clip once without text as a warm-up before new material.

Speed: if the clip is too fast, use 0.8× in YouTube/podcast apps for steps 5–6, then 1.0×. Never shadow below 0.8× — the rhythm becomes unnatural.

## 8. Daily listening routine (45 minutes; scale down to 25 on busy days)

| Slot | Time | Activity | Purpose |
|------|------|----------|---------|
| Morning commute | 15 min | Passive listening: Nihongo con Teppei / YUYU podcast at 1.0× | Volume of input, ear for natural speech |
| Study block 1 | 15 min | Shadowing one clip (method §7) | Parsing speed, contractions |
| Study block 2 | 10 min | 1 set of JLPT-style questions (e.g. 5 items from `listening-scripts.md` via TTS, or 新完全マスター聴解 N2 one unit), then check, then **re-listen with the script** and mark every phrase you missed | Test format, error analysis |
| Evening | 5 min | Anki: card front = audio/phrase from today's misses, back = standard form + meaning | Retention |
| Weekly (Sat) | 50 min | Full mock listening section under test conditions (official sample test or `mock-test-N2-A/B.md` 聴解 read by TTS) | Stamina, pacing |
| Weekly (Sun) | 30 min | One Terrace House / drama episode segment with JP subs, then rewatch 5 minutes without subs | Real-speed casual speech |

**Error log**: keep a running list with three columns — what I heard / what was said / why I missed it (vocabulary, contraction, speed, keigo, distracted). After two weeks, the "why" column tells you exactly what to drill.

**Six-month progression**:
- Months 1–2: N3-level input (Teppei, Comprehensible Japanese Intermediate, NHK Easy). Shadow daily. Learn all 60 contractions cold.
- Months 3–4: N2-level (YUYU, NHK regular news headlines, 新完全マスター聴解 N2, official sample audio). Start weekly mock sections.
- Months 5–6: Full mocks weekly, real speed drama/variety without subs, 即時応答 drills daily (target 10/12).

## 10. Test-day listening checklist

- Pencil and eraser ready; mark sheet oriented correctly (listening is a separate sheet/section).
- During the 問題 instructions and example, read ahead: 問題1 options, 問題2 options, 問題5-3 options.
- Never stop to reconsider a previous item. Mark, move on.
- If you missed an item completely, mark any answer at once — blanks score zero, guesses score 25–33%.
- In 問題4, decide the function of the prompt before the options play.
- In 問題5, draw the grid as soon as you hear "〜が四つの〜について説明しています".
- Breathe between 問題. The audio pause before each 問題 is yours.
