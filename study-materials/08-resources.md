# Resources — Textbooks, Apps, Websites, Audio, and How to Register for the JLPT

This file lists everything outside this folder that the plan refers to. Nothing here is required: the course in this folder is self-contained. Use these to add volume (more reading, more listening, more practice items) and to get the official exam-format practice that only the JLPT publisher provides. Prices are approximate Japanese list prices at the time of writing; imported copies cost more.

### Shin Kanzen Master vs Sou Matome

| | 新完全マスター (Shin Kanzen Master) | 日本語総まとめ (Sou Matome) |
|---|---|---|
| Publisher | 3A Network | ASK Publishing |
| Difficulty | At or slightly above real exam level | Slightly below real exam level |
| Explanations | Japanese only (some volumes have brief English/Chinese/Korean glosses in the vocabulary lists) | Japanese with English, Chinese, Korean translations |
| Layout | Grouped by meaning/function; dense pages; many practice questions per unit | One two-page spread per day; 6–8 weeks per book; illustrated |
| Practice items | Many, in exact exam format, with a mock test at the back | Fewer per unit; a short weekly test |
| Grammar volume | Best on the market for N2: contrasts similar patterns (〜にしては vs 〜わりに, 〜ものだ vs 〜ことだ) | Lists patterns with one example each; contrasts are thin |
| Reading volume | Teaches strategy per question type; texts are exam length | Shorter texts; good warm-up, not enough on its own |
| Listening volume | Full scripts, exam-format tasks, natural speed | Simpler audio, slower pace |
| Best for | Learners with a solid N3 who want to pass N2 with margin | Learners who need translations, or a light first pass before 新完全マスター |
| Verdict for this plan | **Primary.** Buy 文法, 読解, 聴解 at minimum. | **Optional.** 文法 or 漢字 as a warm-up if 新完全マスター is too hard in week 15. |

Other books worth knowing (not required):

- **A Dictionary of Basic / Intermediate / Advanced Japanese Grammar** (Makino & Tsutsui, The Japan Times) — the best English reference for any pattern in `04-grammar/`. Basic + Intermediate cover N5–N2.
- **Kanji in Context** (Japan Times) — if you want more example compounds per kanji than `02-kanji/` gives.
- **速読の日本語** / **中級から学ぶ日本語** (Kenkyusha) — extra N3–N2 reading if you finish `05-reading/` early.
- **TRY! N2** (ASK) — grammar in short story context with audio; a good middle ground between Sou Matome and 新完全マスター.

## 5. How to register for the JLPT

**Test dates.** The JLPT is held twice a year, on the **first Sunday of July** and the **first Sunday of December**, in Japan and at overseas sites. Some overseas countries hold only one of the two sittings — check the site list on jlpt.jp for your country. For this plan (started mid-September 2026) the target is the **July 2027** sitting; the December 2026 sitting is too early.

**Registration windows.** Registration opens about three months before the test and lasts roughly a month. The exact dates differ by country, but as a rule:

| Sitting | Registration window (approximate) | Results |
|---|---|---|
| July (first Sunday) | **Late March to mid/late April** | Late August online; certificates by post in September–October |
| December (first Sunday) | **Late August to mid/late September** | Late January online; certificates by post in February–March |

Registration closes hard: there is no late registration and no waiting list. Put the opening date in your calendar the moment it is published (usually January for July and June for December) because popular overseas sites fill up in days.

**Where to register.**

- **In Japan:** online only, via **MyJLPT** (info.jees-jlpt.jp). Create a MyJLPT account first (do this early — the account itself can be created any time). During the window, log in, choose level and preferred test area, upload a photo, and pay the fee (¥7,500 for all levels as of 2025; check the current figure). The test voucher (受験票) is mailed about two to three weeks before the test.
- **Outside Japan:** through the **local host institution** in each country (a Japan Foundation office, a university, or a Japanese cultural association). jlpt.jp lists every overseas test site with a link to the host's own page. Registration methods vary — some are online, some still require a paper form, a photo and a bank transfer or postal payment. Fees are set locally (in India, for example, roughly ₹1,700–2,000 depending on level and city; in the US about $100; in the UK about £100). The voucher and instructions come from the host institution, not from jlpt.jp.

**Choosing the level.** You register for one level per sitting and cannot change it after the window closes. If you are between N3 and N2 in month 5, register for N2 anyway if your Mock A estimate is 80+/180; a fail at N2 is still a useful score report. If Mock A is under 70, N3 in July and N2 in December is the safer path.

**What you need.**

- A valid passport or national photo ID (the same one you will bring on test day; the name must match).
- A recent passport-style photo (digital upload for MyJLPT; physical photos for some overseas hosts).
- An email address you check regularly, and the fee.

## 6. Test-day rules (official)

- **Bring:** the test voucher (受験票), the ID you registered with, several **HB or No. 2 pencils** (mechanical pencils are allowed in Japan but some overseas sites forbid them — check your voucher), a plain **eraser**, and an ordinary **wristwatch** (analogue or digital, no smart functions). Nothing else is allowed on the desk.
- **Not allowed:** dictionaries, notes, phones (must be switched fully off and put away — a phone that rings is grounds for expulsion), smartwatches, earphones, calculators, pens or ballpoints (answers must be in pencil for the OMR sheet), rulers, and pencil cases on the desk.
- **Sections and timing (N2):** 言語知識（文字・語彙・文法）・読解 105 minutes, then a break, then 聴解 50 minutes. You cannot go back to a section once its time ends. The listening section plays each item **once**.
- **Answer sheet:** mark-sense (OMR). Fill the circle completely; erase cleanly. Marks outside the circle or two marks on one item score zero. There is **no penalty for wrong answers** — never leave an item blank.
- **Lateness:** you will not be admitted after the section has started (in Japan, admission is refused after the start of the instructions for that section). Arrive 30 minutes before the reporting time on your voucher.
- **Leaving early:** not allowed during the listening section; during the first section, only if the invigilator permits, and you cannot return.
- **Photo and ID check:** proctors compare your face, voucher photo and ID. Wear nothing that hides your face.
- **Results:** score report shows section scores (0–60 each), the total (0–180), pass/fail, and a "reference information" grade (A/B/C) for 文字・語彙 and 文法 separately. **Pass = 90/180 overall and at least 19/60 in each of the three sections.** Certificates do not expire.

## 7. Exam-day checklist

Print this, tick it the night before.

**The night before**
- [ ] Voucher (受験票) printed or in the required form, photo attached if required
- [ ] Photo ID (the one used at registration), in a wallet you will actually carry
- [ ] 4–5 sharpened HB pencils (or mechanical pencil + spare leads if allowed on your voucher) and 2 erasers
- [ ] Wristwatch set to the correct time (not a smartwatch)
- [ ] Route to the test site checked, including the exact building and room number on the voucher; know the reporting time (集合時間), not just the start time
- [ ] Water bottle and a small snack for the break (consumed outside the exam room)
- [ ] Light review only: `04-grammar/` confusable pairs, `06-listening/listening-guide.md` contractions list. No new material, no full mock.
- [ ] In bed by a time that gives 7–8 hours of sleep

**Morning**
- [ ] Breakfast with protein; no more caffeine than you are used to
- [ ] Phone fully charged for the journey, then switched **off** (not silent) before entering the room
- [ ] Leave to arrive 30–40 minutes before the reporting time
- [ ] Ten minutes of Japanese audio on the way (a podcast you know well), to get your ear switched on

**In the room**
- [ ] Voucher and ID on the desk as instructed; everything else in the bag under the chair
- [ ] Write your registration number and name exactly as printed on the voucher, as soon as told
- [ ] 言語知識・読解 (105 min): 文字・語彙 15 min → 文法 20 min → 読解 65 min → 5 min to check that every item has exactly one mark. Do 問題14 (情報検索) before 問題13 if you are short on time — it is faster per point.
- [ ] If an item stalls you for more than 90 seconds, mark your best guess, note the number on the question paper, and move on
- [ ] 聴解 (50 min): read the printed choices for 問題1 and 問題2 while the instructions play; for 問題4, look at nothing — just listen; for 問題5, take notes on the question paper
- [ ] Never leave a blank; there is no penalty for guessing

**After**
- [ ] Write down, from memory, which 問題 felt weakest — before you forget. Results come about seven weeks later; if you need to re-sit, that note is where your next study block starts.
