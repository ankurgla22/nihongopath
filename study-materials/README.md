# Japanese N2 in 6 Months — Complete Self-Study Course

This folder is a full curriculum from zero Japanese to JLPT N2. Everything you need to study is here: schedule, kana, kanji, vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening, and mock tests.

## Read this first: the honest numbers

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Typical hours needed for N2 from zero | 1,500–2,200 hours |
| Days in 6 months | ~180 |
| Hours per day this plan requires | 5–6 hours, 6 days a week (about 900–1,000 hours) |
| Kanji to learn | ~1,000 |
| Vocabulary to learn | ~5,000 |
| Grammar points to learn | ~500 |

This plan compresses what most learners do in 18–24 months into 6. It works only if you are consistent: **5–6 focused hours every day, no week off**. If you can only manage 2–3 hours a day, follow the same plan but expect it to take 10–12 months. Nobody can guarantee an exam result, but a learner who completes every item in this plan and scores 65%+ on the mock tests in month 6 is well positioned to pass.

**JLPT sittings**: July and December. Today is mid-September 2026. The December 2026 sitting is too soon. Your target exam is **July 2027** (registration roughly March–April 2027 at jlpt.jp or your local test centre). That gives you 6 months of core study plus a buffer of review time, which is exactly what you want.

## How to study from these files

1. **Follow the schedule.** Open the master schedule each Monday. It tells you exactly which kanji day-sets, vocab ranges, and grammar numbers to cover.
2. **Use spaced repetition for kanji and vocab.** Install Anki (free). Every day, add the day's kanji and words as cards (front: word, back: reading + meaning). Review Anki every morning without exception. This is the single most important habit.
3. **Grammar: read, copy, produce.** For each grammar point: read the explanation, copy the three examples by hand, then write two sentences of your own.
4. **Listening cannot be skipped.** Listening is a third of the score and the section most self-learners fail. Do the daily listening block from week 4 onward.
5. **Take the tests on schedule.** The N3 checkpoint in week 17 and the N2 mocks in weeks 24 and 26 tell you where you stand. Treat weak sections with the review weeks.

## Pass criteria (JLPT N2)

| Section | Max | Section minimum |
|---|---|---|
| 言語知識 (文字・語彙・文法) | 60 | 19 |
| 読解 | 60 | 19 |
| 聴解 | 60 | 19 |
| **Total** | **180** | **90 to pass** |

You must reach 90/180 overall AND at least 19 in every section. A brilliant reading score cannot rescue a failed listening score.
